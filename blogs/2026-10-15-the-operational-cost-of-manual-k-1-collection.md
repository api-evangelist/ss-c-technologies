For family offices, institutional asset managers and funds-of-funds subject to US taxation, tax season isn't a single event. For firms with meaningful exposure to external fund investments, it stretches across months, and the delay usually has little to do with the tax code itself. It has to do with how hard it still is to collect the underlying data.
