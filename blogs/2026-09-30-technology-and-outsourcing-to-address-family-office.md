The family office model allows the world’s wealthiest to institutionalize and centralize the management of their financial and other affairs. It helps structure the family’s asset allocation to meet their investing goals, which may span across asset types and vary from generation to generation. As wealth grows in regions like APAC and the Middle East, family offices are expected to grow as well.
